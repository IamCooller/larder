# What did not work

Every idea this project closed, with the measurement that closed it. The negative results are kept on purpose: they are the part most likely to save someone else the same weeks of work.

## How to read the tables

- There is one table per theme. Columns: the idea; its scope; what was measured (numbers, machine, setup); why it was closed; the date it was closed.
- **Scope.** "MoE only" needs routed experts. "Dense only" applies to dense models only. "Any model" applies to MoE and dense models. "CUDA only" and "Metal only" name one backend. Some rows combine two of these.
- **Machines.** "16 GB M4 MacBook Pro" is written "the M4". "Linux PC, RTX 5060 Ti 16 GB, 15 GB RAM, one NVMe SSD" is written "the PC". A row without a machine is a desk study, a replay of recorded traces, or an analysis of the weights.
- **35B mixed pack.** Qwen3.6-35B-A3B with 20 of its 40 layers' experts at 4-bit and 20 at 2-bit, 16.1 GB. Every 35B number measured on the M4 ran this pack, not the 4-bit pack the launcher installs. The 35B rows measured on the PC say so.
- **Other models.** Flash-Next is Qwen3.8-Flash-Next. "EXL3" is its 3-bit EXL3 expert pack (45.8 GB of experts) and "K4" its 4-bit EXL3 pack (60.9 GB). The 80B is Qwen3-Next-80B-A3B.
- **Prompts.** ru, en and py are the Russian prose, English prose and Python prompts of the standard screen. A time "a token" is the decode time of one token.
- **Engine words.** The "arena" is the fixed resident expert cache of the early engine and the "store" is its later, elastic form. "Staging rows" are spare rows that hold guessed experts. The cache bias (`--cache-bias`) nudges the router toward experts already in memory; at 0 the routing is exact.
- **Measured or estimated.** "(est.)" marks an estimate. Every other number was measured.
- **Bars.** Each screen had a kill bar written before the run. "Bar" in a row is that threshold.
- **Dates.** "<= date" means the idea was closed by that date and the exact day was not recorded.
- **"Code deleted in cleanup B"** means the code was removed in a later cleanup. Its numbers and this record stay.
- **Lossy levers.** Anything that can change a greedy reply beyond bf16 ties was out of scope by the project's exact-only rule, unless it passed a paired GSM8K / HumanEval gate with McNemar not significant. Some lossy ideas were still measured, to know their ceiling.
- **"Reopen if"** names the new argument that would justify measuring the idea again.
- Only measurements made on the M4 or the PC are listed.

What worked is in WORKED.md. The machines, SSD rates and the per-token budget are in HARDWARE.md.
